# Research Methodology

## Objective

Develop systematic methods for investigating financial market behaviour, estimating probabilities, identifying potential inefficiencies, and evaluating opportunities quantitatively.

## Research Process

### 1. Problem Definition
Define a specific financial question or market hypothesis.

### 2. Data Collection
Identify relevant datasets and evaluate their accuracy, completeness, historical coverage, reliability, and potential biases.

### 3. Hypothesis Development
Translate the research question into a measurable hypothesis.

### 4. Quantitative Modelling
Explore appropriate mathematical and statistical methods.

### 5. Expected Value Analysis
Evaluate possible outcomes, their estimated probabilities, and associated costs.

### 6. Backtesting
Test the research hypothesis against historical data. Avoid look-ahead bias, survivorship bias, and data leakage.

### 7. Validation
Evaluate results using appropriate measures, such as win rate, expected value, profit factor where applicable, maximum drawdown, calibration, statistical significance, and out-of-sample performance.

### 8. Continuous Improvement
Document findings, identify weaknesses, and refine the research methodology.

## Research Standards

- Never assume historical performance guarantees future results.
- Avoid overfitting.
- Include realistic transaction costs.
- Separate training and testing datasets.
- Document assumptions.
- Report negative results as well as positive results.
